## File conventions

Use lowercase, hyphenated filenames:

```text
day-01-speaker-surname-short-title.pdf
day-02-speaker-surname-short-title.pdf
```

Source slide files may be included when the presenter wishes to share them. Keep exported PDFs next to the source files and add a short entry to the relevant day's `README.md`.

Put visuals specific to this section in `Figures/`. Shared introductory visuals belong in the top-level `Assets/` folder.

Every upload should state:

- title and presenter;
- session date;
- a one- or two-sentence abstract;
- software or data requirements, if any;
- the licence or reuse permission chosen by the presenter; and
- a preferred citation or DOI, when available.
