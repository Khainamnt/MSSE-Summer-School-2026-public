# School programme

**1–4 September 2026 · Online**

The detailed session timetable, speaker assignments, and joining information will be added as they are confirmed. Times should include a time zone.

## Day 1 — Introduction to Molecular Simulations for Subsurface Engineering

Foundations, modelling choices, simulation workflows, and applications to subsurface engineering.

## Day 2 — Interfacial Phenomena in Subsurface Systems

Interfaces, wetting, adsorption, capillarity, and molecular-scale behaviour in porous materials.

## Day 3 — Rock–Fluid Interactions at Nanoscale under Reservoir Conditions

Mineral–fluid interactions, confinement, reservoir conditions, and implications for subsurface processes.

## Day 4 — Transport Phenomena in Bulk and Confinement

Diffusion, transport properties, confinement effects, analysis, validation, and multiscale interpretation.

## Session formats

- **Invited talks:** expert research presentations on molecular simulations in subsurface environments
- **Tutorials:** focused discussions of fundamental concepts
- **Hands-on sessions:** guided implementation and application of simulation methods
